While a menu or the participant editor is open, the first press elsewhere only closes it; the next press acts. For example, with a gap's insert menu open, a click on a participant or a message, or a right-click on another gap, now only closes the menu. Escape closes whichever menu, editor or drag is open from one place, and releasing the mouse right after Escape no longer completes a cancelled message reorder.
