ZenUML typing trial

We are testing a change to ZenUML: while you type a sequence diagram, the diagram draws new arrows and boxes with a short animation. We want to check that this does not slow people down or make changes harder to notice. The trial takes about 15 minutes. You need a computer with a keyboard.

What you will do

  • A free warm-up in an editor with suggestions.
  • Three short typing tasks: copy a text, with a 3-minute limit each, and answer two quick questions about the diagram.

What is recorded

  • What you type in the editors on this page, with the time of each edit.
  • Which version of the animation you saw, in which order, and whether the device has a keyboard or is touch-only.
  • Whether you pasted text, how long the tab was hidden, how long each task took, and your answers to the questions.
  • A random session code. It is not linked to you.

What is not recorded

  • No IP address, no name, no email address.

How long it is kept

The data is deleted after 90 days.

What it is used for

Only to decide whether the animation is released. Results will be published as numbers, not as individual recordings.
